About Us

Located alongside the National Wild and Scenic Kern River, just two and ½ miles upstream of historic Kernville, California, Falling Waters is a rustic river resort that has welcomed guests for decades.

Most of our rooms are pet friendly. Enjoy our manicured grounds, fishing right from your room, and our crystal blue swimming pool. Whether you're looking for a quiet getaway or a base for exploring the Kern River Valley, you'll find a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere here.

We look forward to hosting you.

About Falling Waters

Located alongside the National Wild and Scenic Kern River, just two and ½ miles upstream of historic Kernville, California, our rustic river resort awaits your pleasure.

The moment you step into the resort office you'll admire the attention to detail and comfort that you'd expect from the finest hostels anywhere. But our exquisite, tastefully furnished rooms and low budget saving rates will amaze you even more.

Most rooms, but not all are pet friendly so bring your furry friend along for the adventure. We have something for everyone, and every age, in your family or group. You can roam the vast manicured grounds; drop your line, a few short steps from your room, for delectable rainbow trout; or just relax and take in the sun at our crystal blue swimming pool.

But that's just the calm before the rapids. A vast choice of nature's excitement and options surround the resort. Many of our guests are here for outdoor recreation and adventure, and Falling Waters is right in the middle of the action. If you're searching for fantastic fishing, whitewater rafting and kayaking thrills, rock climbing, trail hiking, mountain biking, or gazing upon the beautiful grandeur of the Kern River Valley and Sequoia National Forest, our resort is the ideal rendezvous and evening retreat for you and your group.

Some of our guests are here for the casual rest and relaxation. Some prefer browsing the quaint antique and gift shops in Kernville. No matter what your goals are, winter, spring, summer, or fall… Falling Waters is your answer!

Things to Do in Kern Valley

Falling Waters is the perfect base for adventure and relaxation in one of California's most beautiful river valleys.

🎣

Rainbow Trout Fishing

Drop your line just a few steps from your room into the Kern River for some of California's best rainbow trout fishing. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a beginner, the river offers excellent opportunities year-round.

🚣

Whitewater Rafting & Kayaking

Experience the thrill of running the rapids on the scenic Kern River. Various outfitters in Kernville offer guided rafting and kayaking trips for all skill levels.

🥾

Trail Hiking

Explore stunning trails through Sequoia National Forest and the Kern River Valley. Trails range from easy nature walks to challenging mountain hikes with breathtaking views.

🧗

Rock Climbing

The Kern Valley is a renowned rock climbing destination with world-class climbing areas suitable for climbers of all skill levels. Guided climbing tours are available.

🚴

Mountain Biking

Ride challenging singletrack and scenic forest roads through the beautiful Kern River Valley and Sequoia National Forest with trails for every ability.

🚗

Scenic Drives

Take in the grandeur of the Kern River Valley and Sequoia National Forest from your car. Several scenic drives offer stunning views and roadside stops.

Historic Kernville

Just a short drive away, the charming town of Kernville offers antique shops, gift boutiques, restaurants, and local history. Browse unique finds and enjoy the laid-back mountain town atmosphere that visitors have loved for generations.
